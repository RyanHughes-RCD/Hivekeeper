Parts on the bench

These are the source sprites, at their real pixel sizes. Paired parts (eyes, antennae, legs, spines, wings) are drawn once as the left half and mirrored. The stitcher layers them back to front, adds shading from the top left, then traces one outline around the whole silhouette. Magic is drawn last and left unoutlined so it reads as light.
